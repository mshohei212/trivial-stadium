[Full Description (Max 4000 chars)]
Trivial Stadium is a fast-paced, turn-based tactical arena battle RPG where you face increasingly powerful waves of monsters in gladiatorial combat.

Manage your HP and limited recovery potions wisely to survive each encounter and claim victory in the stadium.

Key Combat Features:
- Normal Attack: Deal reliable damage with a chance to land a critical hit.
- Heavy Strike: Unleash a devastating blow with a boosted critical hit rate.
- Use Potion: Use your limited supply of potions at the right moment to recover HP and turn the tide of battle.

Highlights:
- Quick, strategic turn-based battles designed for pick-up-and-play sessions
- Progressive monster difficulty across multiple arena waves
- Integrated with Google Play Games Services to track your combat milestones and Game Stats in real time

Step into the arena and prove your tactical mastery in Trivial Stadium!
